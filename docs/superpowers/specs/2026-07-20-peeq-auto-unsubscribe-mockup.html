The safety interlock, when it fires

Auto-unsubscribe paused. YouTube access is unhealthy (cookie status: stale), so channel failures can't be trusted to mean a channel is really gone. No channel will be unsubscribed until access is restored.

What both options assume

  • Dead ≠ dormant. Only deleted, seen on 3 consecutive scans, auto-unsubscribes. members, age and geo describe restricted content on a living channel and must never trigger it.
  • Nothing is destroyed. Unsubscribe deletes only the subscriptions row — the channel, its scan history and every archived video survive, so re-subscribe genuinely restores it. Both mockups say so on the row, because an automatic action you don't trust is worse than no automation.
  • Dormant is never automatic. 6 months with no new discovery only ever raises a flag.
  • The interlock is load-bearing. If the cookie goes stale, every channel could fail at once — without the interlock a single bad cookie could wipe the whole subscription list.
