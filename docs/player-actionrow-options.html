The line from Keep forever to the category pill

Six controls, four different heights, two hairlines and one pill left stranded at the far end. Below: what the row is today, then five ways to settle it. Every row is drawn at its real pixel size, in the app's own colours — nothing here is scaled or stylised.

The sleep bug is already fixed

The sleep menu was a plain absolutely-positioned child of its pill, pinned top: calc(100% + 7px) with no idea where the viewport ended. On an iPad the action row sits about 100px above the fold, so six presets opened straight off the bottom of the screen.

Now: a shared useMenuPlacement measures once per open and flips the menu upward when there is more room above than below. The category picker and the ⋮ menu use the same hook — they had the identical flaw. .sleepmenu also gained the height clamp .catmenu has carried all along. This is independent of everything below: whichever option you pick, the fix survives it.

Five ways out

01

One height, one silhouette

smallest change

Change nothing about what the row contains or the order it sits in. Give every control the same 34px height and the same rounded shape, drop the second hairline, and push the category pill to the right edge so its distance reads as intended rather than leftover.

Option 01

What changes

  • Every control is 34px tall with a 17px radius — one family, one baseline.
  • The second hairline goes; margin-left: auto does that job better.
  • No control moves, appears or disappears. No behaviour changes at all.

What it costs

  • watch .metapill is shared with Library cards. The override must be scoped .playacts .metapill or the whole grid moves with it.
  • The row still reads as six loose things. This tidies the mess; it does not resolve it.
  • Buttons shrink from 40px to 34px — still above the 32px comfortable-tap floor, but it is a real reduction on touch.
02

What it is, then what this sitting does

Give the row a rule you can state in one sentence. On the left, the controls that change the video permanently — kept, watched, filed. On the right, pushed to the edge, the ones that only affect this sitting: subtitles, sleep, and the overflow. One hairline disappears because the gap now carries the split.

Option 02

What changes

  • The category pill joins the two toggles — it belongs with them: all three say what the video is, and all three outlive the page.
  • Sleep, subtitles and ⋮ move to the right edge as one cluster. Nothing was added or taken away.
  • Both hairlines go. The gap between the clusters is the divider.
  • Shown here with the timer armed, so you can see the countdown pill at its widest — the row must not reflow when it arms.

What it costs

  • The category pill jumps left. It is documented at Player.tsx:1219 as belonging with the actions "behind a separator" — that comment gets rewritten, not ignored.
  • Same scoped-.metapill caution as 01.
  • On a narrow window the row wraps and margin-left: auto stops meaning anything; the two clusters need to stack deliberately rather than by accident.
03

The category goes back up to the facts

The row's real problem may be that it holds one thing that is not an action. Move the category onto the byline, beside the channel and the dates, where the rest of what-this-video-is already lives. It stays fully editable — it is a button up there too. What is left below is five controls and one hairline.

Option 03

What changes

  • The category becomes an 11px pill on the byline, one chevron wide, still a real picker.
  • The action row drops to five controls, one hairline, and the last thing in it is the ⋮ — which is where an eye expects a row of actions to end.
  • The category is now visible on the share page and the player in the same place, if you want that parity later.

What it costs

  • argue first This directly reverses the reasoning written at Player.tsx:1219–1222. The counter-argument: editable does not mean action. The Library card's watched chip is editable and lives on the card, not in a button row.
  • An 11px chevron is a small tap target. It needs padding beyond its ink to clear ~32px on touch.
  • The byline already ellipsises long channel names and wraps at narrow widths; a fourth item makes that busier.
04

One toolbar, not six controls

Put everything inside a single bordered shell with internal dividers. Instead of six objects that each need their own edge, there is one object with segments. The chrome moves from the controls to the container, so the controls themselves can go flat and quiet.

Option 04

What changes

  • One border around the lot; the dividers become internal and thin instead of two floating hairlines.
  • Every control loses its own background and border. State is carried by colour alone — gold for kept, terracotta for watched and subtitles-on.
  • The category stops being an outlier: it is simply the last segment.
  • Grouping is now visible without a separator doing extra work, so the three groups can be read at a glance.

What it costs

  • The biggest new pattern here — nothing else in Peeq is a segmented toolbar, so it arrives without precedent to lean on.
  • Flat controls inside a box read as less tappable. The hover and pressed states have to work harder, and there is no rest-state border to hint at hit area.
  • A fixed shell wraps badly. Below roughly 620px it either scrolls sideways or has to break into two shells.
05

Two decisions and a menu

Only two things in that row are decisions you make often: keep it, and mark it watched. Leave those labelled and let everything else fall into the ⋮ menu that already exists — subtitles and sleep included, sleep carrying its countdown as a flag so the armed state stays readable without opening anything.

What changes

  • The row drops to three controls plus the ⋮. Both hairlines are gone with nothing left to separate.
  • Subtitles and Sleep become menu rows with a right-hand flag showing their state — the flag slot the ⋮ menu already uses.
  • The quietest of the five by a distance, and the only one that gets calmer rather than tidier.

What it costs

  • Subtitles goes from one tap to two, on the control most likely to be toggled mid-video. That is the real price here.
  • An armed sleep timer stops being visible on the page. The flag only shows once the menu is open, so "is it still running?" needs a look inside — a countdown badge on the ⋮ would answer it, at the cost of the calm this option is buying.
  • Sleep needs a submenu or an inline preset strip. Neither exists in RowMenu today, so this is more new code than it looks.

Two rules every option was checked against. Nothing here rests invisible until hover — @media (hover: none) is not an escape hatch, since an iPad with a trackpad reports hover: hover. And nothing changes the shared .metapill or .chip rules unscoped: those selectors are used by the Library grid, Decide, Channels and the share page, and restyling them here would move all of them.

They combine. 03 is about where the category lives and 05 is about how much the row holds; either works with 01's single height underneath it. Say which parts you want and I will draw the combination before building anything.
